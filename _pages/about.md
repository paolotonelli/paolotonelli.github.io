## Paolo Tonelli

I’m an M.S. student in Computer Engineering at **UC Santa Barbara (UCSB)**, working on hardware acceleration for scientific machine learning. My research focuses on **FPGA-based inference accelerators**, **tensor-train (TT)–compressed neural operators** (e.g., DeepONet/FNO), and **fault-tolerant/robust** ML under quantization and resource constraints.

Previously, I earned my B.S. in Electrical Engineering at the **University of Padua**, where working on my thesis sparked my current interest in combining numerical methods with efficient, reliable ML hardware.

I enjoy building real systems: I have experience with Xilinx FPGAs, quantization pipelines, and golden-model Python tools that match RTL simulations. Lately I’ve been exploring **SEU/SET fault models**, lightweight redundancy, and error-localization in TT cores for graceful degradation.

---

### Research interests
- **Fault tolerance & reliability** in ML accelerators
- Scientific ML & **neural operators** (DeepONet, FNO)  
- **Model compression** (tensor-train), **quantization**, and HW/SW co-design  
- Memory hierarchies (BRAM/URAM) and PE-array microarchitectures  

---

### Currently 
- Studying **fault models** and mitigation for tensorized networks (selective voting, checksums, adaptive rescaling)  
- Designing and evaluating a **TT-compressed, quantized** inference pipeline on FPGA
